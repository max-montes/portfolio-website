New Grad Google Alert

Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

About this app

New Grad Google Alert is a private, single-user automation owned and operated by Max Montes Soza. It searches Google Careers for early-career software engineering postings and emails matching alerts from and to maxmontessoza@gmail.com. It is not offered as a public service or to additional users.

Google data accessed and why

The app requests the Gmail API scopes gmail.send and gmail.readonly.

  • gmail.send is used only to send job-alert emails.
  • gmail.readonly is used only to search Sent mail for a unique marker associated with an alert. This verifies whether an alert was sent and prevents duplicate alerts.

The app does not read unrelated message contents. It does not retain Gmail message content.

Storage and sharing

OAuth client configuration and the refresh token are stored locally in macOS Keychain. Google user data is transmitted only to Google APIs as necessary to authenticate, send alerts, and verify or deduplicate sent alerts.

The app does not sell or share Google user data, use it for advertising, or disclose it to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

New Grad Google Alert's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and revocation

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Revocation prevents the app from accessing Gmail through the authorized account. Local OAuth credentials can also be removed from macOS Keychain.

Contact

For privacy questions, email maxmontessoza@gmail.com.
